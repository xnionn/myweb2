After the last chapter

AS

Aliya Sadykova

26 September 2026

I reread Dandelion Wine after a long break. At school, all I remembered was the summer. This time, it was the adults' conversations and the way Douglas tries to hold on to everything that stayed with me.

One question for our next meeting: does this feel like a happy book or a sad one to you? I think the answer changes as you get older.
